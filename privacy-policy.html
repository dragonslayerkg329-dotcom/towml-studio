Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Important: TOWML STUDIO develops different games with different features. The information processed can vary by game, platform, and the services enabled for that title. This policy must be checked and updated for each release before publication.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TOWML STUDIO (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use games and services developed or published by TOWML STUDIO (“Services”). We aim to collect only information that is reasonably needed to operate, maintain, secure, and support the Services.

2. Information We May Process

The information involved depends on the game and its features:

  • Account information: Some games may offer accounts or sign-in. If enabled, information such as a username, email address, account identifier, or sign-in information may be processed to create and manage the account. Games without account features do not require you to create a TOWML STUDIO account to play.
  • Gameplay and technical information: Depending on the game, platform, and technical services used, limited information such as game settings, progress, device and operating-system details, app version, crash reports, or diagnostic records may be processed to provide and troubleshoot the game.
  • Support communications: If you contact us, we will process the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message, to respond.
  • Platform information: App stores and platform providers may process information under their own privacy policies when you download, install, or use a game through their services.

3. Analytics

Our use of analytics tools has not yet been finalized across all games. Analytics may differ by title. Before a game uses analytics, its applicable privacy information should identify the analytics tools or providers, the categories of information involved, the purposes, and any available choices or controls. Do not assume that every TOWML STUDIO game uses analytics.

4. How We Use Information

Where applicable, information may be used to provide game features, maintain gameplay and saved progress, diagnose errors, protect the Services, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. We do not use information for a purpose that is incompatible with the purpose explained to you.

5. In-Game Purchases

As of the effective date, TOWML STUDIO games do not offer in-game purchases. If this changes, we will update the applicable privacy information and explain any relevant payment processing before introducing purchase features. App stores may still process information under their own policies.

As of the effective date, TOWML STUDIO games do not provide multiplayer features. If a game later adds multiplayer, chat, user-generated content, or other player-to-player features, we will update the applicable privacy information before those features are made available.

We may use service providers where needed to host, distribute, maintain, secure, or support a game. Such providers may process information on our behalf or under their own privacy terms, depending on the service. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Services, or our legal rights. We do not sell personal information.

8. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our games are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. Where a game is directed to children or may be used by children, we will apply the notices, consent requirements, and safeguards required by the laws that apply to that game and audience. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request before responding.
